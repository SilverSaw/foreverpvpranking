How to read this

  • Counter score is the average of how hard each enemy race/class/spec beats you, weighted by how common it is on the other faction in the beta capture (226,453 characters). 0 means nobody counters you.
  • Main threats lists up to three enemy specs whose average edge over you is +0.10 or more, biggest pressure first, with that edge in brackets. Near-ties below +0.10 are left out. On this scale +0.50 is a hard counter.
  • Hard counters is the share of enemy players whose matchup against you is clearly favourable.
  • Rank range comes from rerunning the model 2,000 times with matchup values shifted by up to ±0.25, spec and racial strength scaled 0.5–1.5×, and random enemy spec splits. A narrow bar means the rank holds up; a wide one means it depends on assumptions.
  • Matchups are Classic-style class knowledge adjusted for Forever's spec kits (Blizzard class deep dives, Oct 15 beta notes) and PvP-relevant racial effects only. Enemy specs are assumed to be split evenly within each class, since the capture has no spec data.
  • Racials are scored from the in-game tooltips, updated with the final racial patch notes of Oct 26, 2026 and Blizzard's Oct 27 additions (Ruthless 1% crit, Human Spirit 10%, Dwarf Artisan's Precision +1% hit, Tauren Endurance health only, Orc Command +5% pet damage counted by each spec's pet share, Orc Blood Fury on a 2 min cooldown, Troll Berserking 15 s, Undead Touch of the Grave 15 s internal cooldown, Skyborne Wind Blessed 2% haste). Skysight's Charged buff lasts an hour, survives death and only drops on entering a raid, so it counts fully in world PvP and for half of players in battlegrounds. Passive bonuses count in every fight. Cooldown racials (Elune's Light, Blood Fury, Berserking, Eureka!) count fully in a single world fight but are limited by their cooldown in battlegrounds. Eureka! only boosts non-periodic damage, Will to Survive counts at 75% for its 1.5 s global cooldown, and Big Game Hunter and Beast Slaying assume Druid animal forms count as Beasts. Priest racial spells (Desperate Prayer, Chastise, Contingency Plan, Confounding Flash, Elune's Grace, Starshards, Feedback, Touch of Weakness, Hex of Weakness, Shadowguard) use the 16 Oct beta-client values.
  • The beta caps at level 30 with no battleground queue, so no real PvP results exist yet. Fewest counters is not the same as strongest: a warrior with a pocket healer is stronger in a battleground than this table suggests.

Sources: Oct 15 beta notes, racial abilities, Blizzard deep dives (Priest & Warrior, Rogue & Warlock, Hunter & Druid, Paladin), Seemeta PvP tier list, Mobalytics dueling tier list.
